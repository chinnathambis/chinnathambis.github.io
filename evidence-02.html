01 — Project Overview

Reimagining QNB Egypt’s digital banking experience.

QNB Egypt’s established mobile and internet banking products needed more than a visual refresh. As the platforms evolved over time, their information architecture, navigation and interaction patterns became increasingly complex.

The opportunity was to rethink how customers accessed and managed their banking services, simplify essential journeys and establish a consistent, scalable digital experience across multiple customer segments.

The engagement focused on transforming the existing digital banking ecosystem while creating a design foundation that could support future growth.

02 — The Challenge

An established banking ecosystem that needed a fundamental rethink.

QNB Egypt’s mobile and internet banking experiences had evolved over approximately a decade. They supported a broad range of banking services — but had accumulated layers of navigation, complex information structures and interaction patterns that made everyday tasks harder to discover and complete.

The challenge went beyond visual inconsistency: how could a large collection of banking services be organised, how did customers move between tasks, and how could the experience stay intuitive across different customer segments?

Core challenges
01

Complex information architecture

The products contained extensive functionality, but the organisation and hierarchy of features made discoverability challenging.

02

Complicated customer journeys

Everyday tasks such as transfers, bill payments and account management presented opportunities to simplify navigation and interactions.

03

Outdated interaction patterns

Some screens relied on older interface conventions, dense layouts and inconsistent interaction structures.

04

Functional complexity

The experience needed to accommodate numerous banking services without overwhelming customers.

05

Multi-segment experience

The system needed to support distinct customer segments while keeping a coherent underlying experience.

06

Cross-platform consistency

The foundation needed to support mobile, internet banking and the proposed tablet experience.

04 — Research Synthesis

Turning observations into design opportunities.

Rather than treating each module as an isolated UI problem, the team looked for the broader patterns affecting discoverability, information hierarchy and task completion.

UX

UX takeaways

  • Simplify information architecture and feature discovery
  • Improve navigation hierarchy
  • Reduce unnecessary steps in important journeys
  • Make frequent banking actions easier to access
  • Introduce more contextual interactions
  • Improve presentation of account and transaction information
  • Organise complex functionality through clearer grouping
VISUAL

Visual design takeaways

  • Stronger typography and visual hierarchy
  • Better spacing and content organisation
  • Clearer separation between functional sections
  • Consistent components and interaction patterns
  • Improved iconography and action recognition
  • A coherent visual language across modules
CX

Customer experience opportunities

  • A more useful dashboard
  • Financial information that is easier to understand
  • Better account and card visibility
  • Simpler transfers and bill payments
  • A consistent experience across segments

06 — UX Strategy & Experience Design

Rethinking the way customers navigate everyday banking.

The UX work addressed the structural challenges found in discovery. Instead of redesigning screens in isolation, the team considered how information, navigation and customer journeys could work together as one coherent experience.

Key design focus areas
Information architecture
Reconsidering how banking features and services are organised to improve findability and reduce navigation complexity.
Dashboard experience
Improving the hierarchy of financial information, banking services, quick actions and relevant account details.
Accounts
Exploring clearer account summaries, grouping, currency information, transaction visibility and contextual actions.
Transfers
Clearer categorisation, beneficiary access, improved task flow and more focused interactions.
Bill payments
Better service discovery and categorisation, with clearer hierarchy between service categories and payment actions.
Cards
Improving card presentation, information hierarchy and access to card-management actions.
Other banking modules
Applying consistent interaction and information-design principles to deposits, loans, investments, rewards, profile and settings.

07 — Visual Design Direction

Translating QNB’s established brand into a modern digital experience.

The visual transformation was guided by the QNB Group corporate brand guidelines, which QNB Egypt follows and provided to the project. The team’s work was translating that established identity into a coherent digital product language — applying its colour palette, typography, iconography and visual principles to reusable interface components and segment themes.

The QNB corporate brand identity, logo and brand guidelines were created by QNB Group. This work covers their application to QNB Egypt’s digital products.

10 — Leadership & Delivery

Leading the design process from exploration to delivery.

As Design Lead and Senior Product Designer, I balanced hands-on design with team leadership and client collaboration throughout the engagement.

I led a four-member design team while staying actively involved in high-fidelity design, prototyping, design reviews and key UX decisions. Working closely with client stakeholders, I helped translate requirements and feedback into design directions, coordinated internal reviews and managed design progress.

AI-assisted workflow

AI tools were used selectively to speed up parts of the process. Reflection

The engagement reinforced the value of treating complex digital products as interconnected ecosystems rather than isolated screens. A successful transformation needed experience strategy, information architecture, interaction design, visual consistency and a scalable design foundation working together.

It also showed the importance of balancing hands-on design with team leadership, stakeholder collaboration and delivery coordination across a long engagement.
