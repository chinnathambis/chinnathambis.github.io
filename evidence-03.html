01 — Overview

More than a signal bot.

Northline trades crypto futures automatically from trading signals, and runs as a mobile app with real-time alerts. When a signal arrives, Northline checks it, sizes the trade so a loss stays small, places the order with its stop and targets, and explains every step in plain words.

Plenty of tools already turn alerts into orders. Northline focuses on what they do poorly: protecting the whole account, and reporting results honestly.

02 — The Problem

Placing trades is easy. Protecting the account is the hard part.

Trading accounts are rarely lost to one bad signal. They’re lost to a trade with no stop, a position that’s too big, or a bot that keeps trading through a losing streak.

The real question was what a trader needs before trusting a bot with real money, and how to stay in control of it from a phone.

01

Every trade protected

A stop on the exchange from the moment a position opens, even if the server goes down.

02

Limits that hold

Caps on daily loss, open risk and losing streaks that apply across every trade, not one at a time.

03

Control from a phone

See, adjust and stop everything from a phone, without opening a laptop or the exchange.

04

Honest numbers

Results counted from real closed trades, after fees, with test trades kept out.

03 — Principles

Rules that don’t bend.

Before any screen was designed, six rules were fixed. Every later feature had to fit inside them.

01

Stop first

The stop and targets go on the exchange straight after the fill, so a trade stays protected even if Northline is offline.

02

Risk can only say no

Risk limits can block a new trade. They can never open, change or close one.

03

Trade-only keys

Exchange keys can trade but never withdraw, and are locked to Northline’s own server.

04

Tested before real money

Every rule ships with a test built from a real signal example, and runs on practice money first.

05

Honest by default

Stats use real closed trades only, after fees. Test trades never mix in.

06

One-tap exit

A single confirmed tap closes every position and cancels every order.

04 — How It Works

From alert to protected trade, in seconds.

Every signal source uses one shared format, so adding a new source doesn’t change anything else.

01

Signal arrives

A trading signal reaches Northline privately. Repeats of the same signal are ignored.

02

Checked

The entry must sit inside its zone, the stop on the correct side and the targets in order. Anything else is rejected, with the reason.

03

Risk engine

Daily loss, trades open, total open risk, losing streak and drop from peak are checked, plus guards for fees and tiny positions.

04

Sized and placed

Each trade is sized so a loss costs a fixed share of the account. The stop and four targets go on the exchange, and the stop moves to the entry price after the first target.

05

Reported

Every step arrives as an alert in plain words. Open trades are re-checked every few seconds, and a watchdog flags anything that stops answering.

05 — The App

Everything in one app

Alerts tell you what just happened; the app shows everything else. Only the account owner can open it.

Home
Balance and its curve, auto-trading status, what fees cost, what happened to every signal, and Close everything.
Trades
Open trades with live price, stop and target progress, and a red line only when something needs attention.
Signals
Every signal and what Northline did with it: traded, skipped and why, or still running.
Setup
Risk, limits and how trades run, with the dollar amount shown next to every percentage.
06 — Designing in the Open

Every screen agreed before it was built.

Every screen was mocked up, compared with the live version and approved before any code changed. Approved screens, rejected options and the reasons went into a dated design record.

07 — Risk Design

Limits that only ever say no.

The risk limits are the heart of the product. They can stop a new trade, but never touch one that’s already open. Every skipped signal is logged with the reason.

LimitWhat happens
Daily lossNo new trades for the rest of the day
Trades open at onceThe next signal is skipped
Total open riskSkipped if every open stop plus the new one would lose too much
Losses in a rowAuto-trading pauses until the trader resumes it
Drop from peakAuto-trading pauses below a set drop from the best balance
Guards on every signal
Fee guard — first target too close to cover feesToo-small guardNo chasing — limited late entryOpposite signal: skip, or close and reverseKill switch with one confirming tap
08 — Going Live

Switching from practice money to real money

Moving from practice money to real money was designed like any other screen. Every number starts at zero, and practice trades are kept but hidden. Trading pauses if the balance drops too far below its peak, and practice-only tools can’t touch a live account.

Taking a missed trade, safely

If a signal was missed but is still in range, the trader can take it by hand. One screen shows the stop, the money at risk, the size and the margin. The trade then gets the same protection as an automatic one, marked MANUAL.

09 — Brand

A mark that climbs.

The Northline mark is a block N whose diagonal is four even steps, like a staircase or price moving up in steps, drawn as one solid shape. It sits in the app’s own colours: green on near-black, with dark and light versions and an app icon.

It was chosen after several rounds: four first directions, ten more ideas, a folded N, a set of minimal N marks and block-letter studies.

10 — How It Was Built

Designed and directed end to end.

I defined the product, wrote the spec, designed every screen and safety rule, and directed the build with an AI coding agent, reviewing and approving each design and each deploy.

01

Spec first

A product and technical spec set the phases, the risk model, the data model and the security rules before the first line of code.

02

A design record

Every agreed mock kept with its source and reasoning, and the options that weren’t chosen.

03

Tests on every change

Each new rule or message gets a test built from a real signal example; the suite runs on every update.

04

Deploys only on approval

A deploy button runs only after the tests pass, and only with my explicit go-ahead each time.

05

Built to be replaced

The exchange sits behind one connector, so it could change exchanges without touching the rest.

06

Runs on its own

A cloud server with https, nightly backups, a watchdog and a morning status note.

11 — Roadmap & Reflection

From one trader to a product for many.

  6. NowLive on real money

Northline is live and trading real money with its first user. A small friends beta comes next; after that, a paid subscription where each trader connects their own exchange account and keeps their own funds. Northline never holds money or trades on anyone’s behalf.

Reflection

Most of the design work wasn’t about placing trades. It was about the moments when the bot does nothing, and making sure I can always see why.

An AI coding agent made the build fast. The written spec, approved mockups and tests kept it safe.
