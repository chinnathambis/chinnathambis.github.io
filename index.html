AI × Design Lab

One loop · who leads each step

AI gives me more ideas to choose from. The decision stays with the designer. This is how I work on every project:

  1. 01Designer leads

    Prompt

    Define the problem: who it’s for, the limits, and what success looks like.

  2. 02AI leads

    Generate

    Many options, fast: layouts, wording, translations and images.

    Figma AIPluginsChatGPT
  3. 03Both

    Evaluate

    AI points out gaps in flows and wording. I check each option against real data and tricky cases.

  4. 04Designer decides

    Decide

    Pick one direction, and keep the rejected ones to show why.
