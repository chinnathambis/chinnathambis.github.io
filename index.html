AI × Design Lab

Prompt → generate → evaluate → decide

AI widens the option space. The decision stays with the designer. Three places where that split shows up in my work:

AI→

Synthesises interview transcripts into candidate themes — fast, and at a volume I couldn’t match by hand.

Human→

Discards the themes that are true but useless. Chases the contradictions the summary smoothed over.

Decision

Comes from the friction between the two — not from either one alone.

AI→

Generates many variations of a flow or component quickly — more directions than I’d sketch in the same time.

Human→

Tests each against the real constraints: the data, the edge cases, the system it has to live inside.

Decision

One direction — with the rejected ones kept as evidence of why.

AI→

Acts as a tireless first reviewer — flags inconsistencies, missing states, accessibility gaps.

Human→

Decides which critique matters for this user in this moment, and which is noise.

Decision

A design that has been argued with before anyone else sees it.
